# Third-party integration

- [earthtojake/text-to-cad](https://github.com/earthtojake/text-to-cad): MIT-licensed repository; integrated through `cadgen==0.5.1`, the package pinned by the repository's CAD skill at inspection time. The application uses cadgen's build123d re-export, AssemblyHelper, and srgb utilities. Dependency source and applicable notices remain in the installed packages. This project is not affiliated with LEAP 71 or the text-to-cad author.
- [build123d](https://github.com/gumyr/build123d): parametric solid construction and STEP import/export, backed by Open CASCADE.
- [Gmsh](https://gmsh.info/): finite-element meshing. Review its license and commercial licensing options before distributing a proprietary bundled runtime.
- Three.js: browser rendering. SciPy/NumPy: finite-element matrix assembly and solution.

No Noyron source, trained model, or proprietary LEAP 71 engineering knowledge has been included.
